Asset manager reference model · SM-01 Distribution Performance

Five answers, one certified number

"Net flows, last quarter" used to come back five ways. For each of the five, this page shows the definition that now settles it, where that definition is captured in the taxonomy and the data products, how the semantic model references it, and which input rows it acts on. The rail under the diagram places every column in a Databricks Unity Catalog catalog, so the same picture reads as the deployment.

Trace one of the five answers

Each team's number came from a definition choice. Pick a team to light where that choice is now made once, where it is applied, and which published line carries it. The definition cards below the diagram give the detail.

The one rule. A data product assembles what the Data Domains master; it never remasters them. A certified number is defined in one product only, and every other product, scorecard, payout and assistant reads it from there. That is why the diagram has one box where the rule is applied and many boxes that only read.

The definitions, and where each one lives

Every card reads the same way across: the taxonomy attribute that holds the rule, the data product table and column that capture it, the semantic model element that references it, and the input rows it acts on. Identifiers are the model's own.

The certified measure

Net Flows (Net Sales), MSR-NETFLOW

Measure Definition · certified
Taxonomy

TX-MEA-004 Business Definition "Gross sales less redemptions, including net exchanges, excluding reinvestments." TX-MEA-034 adds the alignment: equals ICI net new cash flow where exchanges are included and reinvestments excluded, and EFAMA net sales.

TX-MEA-005 Calculation Formula Gross Sales + Exchanges In − Redemptions − Exchanges Out. TX-MEA-008 Sign Convention: outflows negative.

TX-MEA-006 / 007 Included Purchase, Exchange In, Transfer In (new owner), SMA Contribution, ETF Creation. Excluded Reinvestment, Share Class Conversion.

TX-MEA-010 Time Basis Trade Date. TX-MEA-011 Industry alignment Aligned with Adjustments. TX-MEA-012 Certified Y. Owner: Head of Distribution Analytics.

Data product

DP-008 dp_008.measure_definition: one row per definition version, 16 columns, carrying the business definition, formula, included and excluded types, time basis and the seven Flow Treatment Rule options.

dp_008.measure_value: the computed figure, keyed measure set × reporting period × dimension combination (channel, distributor node, territory, share class) × data basis × snapshot. Column net_flows.

Semantic model

SM-01 metric Net Flows, aggregation additive, expr: sum(flows_aum.net_flows), source: DP-008 / Net Flows. Nothing else.

Always-filters on flows_aum: one dimension grain (share class; channel; territory; distributor firm), period_type = 'MONTH', and the current locked snapshot that no later locked snapshot supersedes. Default dashboard range: trailing 12 locked months.

Input

DP-006 attribution records, signed by Flow Direction, with a Transaction Type in the included list, assigned to the period by trade_date; attributed plus unattributed equals the source total.

Worked quarter: Gross Sales 240 − Redemptions 65 + Net Exchanges 0 → 175.

Sales · $2.41bn

Counted exchanges in as gross and conversions as rep credit

Exchange Treatment · Share Class Conversion Treatment
Taxonomy

TX-MEA-014 Exchange Treatment, object Flow Treatment Rule. "Whether intra-family exchanges are counted in gross sales and redemptions or only as net exchanges." Code list Treatment Option: Include in Gross, Net Only. Certified value Include in Gross: the buy leg is a gross sale, the sell leg a redemption, so they net in Net Flows.

TX-MEA-016 Share Class Conversion Treatment "Whether conversions between share classes of the same fund are counted as flows." Certified value Exclude at Product Level: counted at share-class level only, zero for the fund.

TX-TRN-045 to 050 Linked Transaction: the contra leg, contra share class, conversion reason and the Same-Owner Indicator.

Data product

DP-006 writes the two legs of every exchange and conversion as a linked_transaction pair and publishes the Same-Owner Movement Flag ("Y when a Linked Transaction has Same-Owner Indicator = Y: an exchange, transfer or conversion that is not new money").

DP-008 measure_definition.exchange_treatment and .share_class_conversion_treatment; the result lands in measure_value.net_exchanges.

Semantic model

Metric Net Exchanges sum(flows_aum.net_exchanges): "net money moved into our share classes from our other share classes; the bridge between Gross Sales less Redemptions and Net Flows."

Gross Sales and Redemptions remain available to Sales as the gross view; Net Flows is the certified net. Rep credit is a Salesperson Credit row in DP-006, read in SM-02, never a flow.

Input

Transfer agent switch orders (ISO 20022 setr.013) arrive as Exchange Out and Exchange In legs; conversions as Share Class Conversion with a Conversion Reason.

Worked quarter: T2 exchange 40 out of Fund A into Fund B → Gross Sales +40, Redemptions 40, Net Exchanges 0. T3 conversion of 15 from Class C to Class I → zero at product level.

Finance · $1.86bn

Settlement date, money market funds out, reinvestments out

Time Basis · Money Market Inclusion
Taxonomy

TX-MEA-010 Time Basis "Date driving period assignment." Code list: Trade Date, Settlement Date, As-Of Date, Processing Date, Valuation Date. One value per measure definition; certified value Trade Date.

TX-MEA-019 Money Market Inclusion "Whether money market funds are included in long-term flow measures." Certified value Exclude (Report Separately). TX-MEA-013 Reinvestment Treatment already excludes reinvestments. So two of Finance's three choices agree with the rule.

TX-TRN-015 Trade Date and TX-TRN-016 Settlement Date are both attributes of Transaction; the model keeps both and uses one.

Data product

DP-008 measure_definition.time_basis and .money_market_inclusion.

DP-006 transaction.trade_date and transaction.settlement_date on every row. DP-008 assigns the period from trade date and publishes money market share classes as their own rows, so Finance's exclusion is a filter on share class, not a second number.

Semantic model

flows_aum.period_end_date joins DP-013 Calendar Date; no relationship assigns a period by settlement date. flows_aum.share_class joins DP-004 Share Class, where the vehicle and asset class live, so the money market slice can be shown or excluded without redefining the metric.

A settlement-date Net Flows would be a second Measure Definition version with its own measure name, published beside the certified one.

Input

Transfer agent and NSCC trades carry both dates; the period follows the trade date.

Worked quarter: T4 (trade Sep 29, settles Oct 1) stays in Q3; T5, 60 into the money market fund, is published on its own share-class rows and excluded from the long-term figure.

Product · $2.08bn

Rolled fund net assets forward, so reinvested distributions counted as sales

Reinvestment Treatment
Taxonomy

TX-MEA-013 Reinvestment Treatment "Whether reinvested dividends and capital gains count as sales or as market movement." Code list Treatment Option; certified value Exclude from Sales (Market Movement). References ICI and Morningstar.

TX-MEA-035 Reinvested Distributions is its own measure, and TX-MEA-038 Market Movement is defined as Ending AUM − Beginning AUM − Net Flows, which includes reinvestments when they are excluded from flows.

Transaction Type Reinvestment ("dividend or capital gain reinvested") is a value of the code list, aligned to ICI flow components.

Data product

DP-006 keeps every Reinvestment row (it is a transaction) with transaction_type = 'Reinvestment'; DP-008's Gross Sales definition reads "reinvested distributions excluded".

DP-008 measure_definition.reinvestment_treatment; the amount lands in measure_value.market_movement, with Reinvested Distributions reported separately.

Semantic model

Metric Market Movement sum(flows_aum.market_movement): "the change in AUM not explained by net flows or FX". Verified question 2 states the identity: Beginning AUM + Net Flows + Market Movement + FX Effect = Ending AUM.

Product's roll-forward still works; it is the sum of four published lines rather than a recomputation.

Input

Transfer agent reinvestment postings on the distribution date, one per account and share class.

Worked quarter: T6, 30 of distributions reinvested across the funds → Market Movement, not Gross Sales; Net Flows unchanged.

Platforms · $2.67bn

Counted platform omnibus totals and the sub-accounts under them

Look-Through Level · Attribution Method · Attribution Share %
Taxonomy

TX-TRN-013 Look-Through Level "Level of visibility the record provides into the underlying investor." Code list: Direct Account, Networked Account, Omnibus Aggregate, Omnibus Sub-Account, Plan Level, Sponsor Program Level, Aggregator Estimate. One value per record. Critical data element; cites DTCC Networking and Omni/SERV.

TX-POS-010 to 014 Omnibus Look-Through: Omnibus Parent Account, Look-Through Source, Look-Through Coverage %, Omnibus Residual Amount. The parent is the control total; the sub-accounts explain it.

Attribution Method (Direct, Rep-Matched, Look-Through, Aggregator, Allocated, Estimated, Manual Override) and TX-ATT-006 Attribution Share % ("100% unless split") make every attribution record sum back to its source.

Data product

DP-006 transaction.look_through_level on every row; its Unattributed Amount metric states the invariant: "attributed + unattributed equals the source total."

DP-007 publishes Look-Through Coverage % ("assets resolved below the omnibus parent / total omnibus parent market value") and the residual. DP-008's distributor firm dimension "includes the unattributed member, so firm rows add up to the firm total."

Semantic model

attributed_transactions joins transactions on the source transaction; metrics Transaction Attribution Rate % and Unattributed Transaction Amount show how much resolved to an advisor and why the rest did not.

flows_aum.distributor_firm rows add to the firm total by construction, so a platform's omnibus and its sub-accounts can never both count.

Input

The transfer agent's omnibus posting and the Omni/SERV activity and position files for the same account; DP-006 de-duplicates across feeds.

Worked quarter: T7, a broker-dealer omnibus purchase of 120 with 300 sub-accounts in the Omni/SERV file → Gross Sales +120 once, attributed by look-through, residual in suspense.

Executive pack · $2.20bn

Hand-adjusted from last quarter's method, with two restatements

Snapshot Type · Snapshot Status · Restatement
Taxonomy

Family TX-SNP Reporting Periods, Snapshots & Restatements. TX-SNP-006 Snapshot Type (Flash, Preliminary, Final, Restated), TX-SNP-008 Snapshot Status (Open, Locked, Superseded), TX-SNP-009 Supersedes Snapshot, TX-SNP-010 Approved By.

TX-SNP-012 Restatement Reason: Late Source Data, Source Correction, Attribution Rule Change, Hierarchy Change, Measure Definition Change, Error. TX-SNP-013 Affected Periods, TX-SNP-014 Net Impact Amount, TX-SNP-015 Compensation Impact Indicator, handed to domain 07 for payouts.

TX-MEA-029 every Measure Value row names its Snapshot.

Data product

DP-008 dp_008.snapshot (reporting period × snapshot type × version) and dp_008.restatement (one row per event). Preliminary by business day 3, locked by business day 8; every locked monthly snapshot since 2015 kept, plus every superseded version.

measure_value.restatement_impact carries the net change to a locked period. DP-006's Late Correction Flag marks the trade that caused it.

Semantic model

Always-filter: the current locked snapshot, excluding any snapshot a later locked one supersedes. Metric Restatement Impact sum(flows_aum.restatement_impact): "how much the current locked figures differ from the figures first locked for the same months."

Honest gap: SM-01 lists "which restatement explains the change in a given month" under what it cannot answer yet, because Affected Periods is free text (GAP-06-005).

Input

A cancel-and-correct after the Final Close Date, a hierarchy or crediting-rule change, or a measure definition change. There is no manual input: an adjustment that is not a restatement row does not exist in the model.

Worked quarter: T8, a 12 redemption with trade date Jun 27 arriving in July → Q2 Restated snapshot, Restatement Impact −12, reason Late Source Data; Q3 stays 175.
